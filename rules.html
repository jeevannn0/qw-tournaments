Baseline rules

Rules that protect the lobby

Event notices can add map, equipment, or round details. They cannot quietly change the published fee, prize, or refund terms.

01
Before registrationEligibility and lineup
  • Use the exact in-game name and Free Fire UID entered during registration.
  • Solo entries contain one player. Squad entries contain exactly four players and no substitute.
  • One player may appear in only one entry per tournament.
  • Players under 18 need parent or guardian approval for participation and payment.
  • A generated reference is not a reservation. Only written organizer confirmation assigns a slot.
02
During competitionFair play and accounts
  • Hacks, scripts, modified clients, macros, teaming, account sharing, and deliberate bug exploitation are prohibited.
  • Use only devices and controls allowed by the event notice. Emulators require explicit approval.
  • Offensive names, harassment, threats, or abusive messages can result in removal.
  • The organizer may request match screenshots or recordings when reviewing credible evidence.
03
Before the lobbyCheck-in and room access
  • Choose the lobby displayed by the open match during registration; use the player or team number assigned by the organizer.
  • After registration, join the match group and follow the organizer’s instructions for your selected lobby.
  • Room ID and password are sent privately to confirmed players or squad captains.
  • Do not share room credentials. Leaking them can cancel an entry without refund.
  • Use the assigned number and follow the ready instructions posted with the lobby details.
  • Individual connection or device failure does not guarantee a restart.
04
Published rewardsMatch-specific rewards

The reward summary displayed on an open match is authoritative for that match cycle.

  • Solo, Squad Battle Royale, and Clash Squad / TDM may use different reward structures.
  • Only results accepted through the organizer’s match record and dispute process qualify for payout.
  • A previous match’s fee or reward does not automatically apply to a newly scheduled match.
05
Squad and TDMFour-player entry
  • Squad Battle Royale and Clash Squad / TDM registration opens only when the matching event page says Registration open.
  • Each squad entry requires a squad name and exactly four players, with player one as captain.
  • No schedule, entry fee, payment request, or reward amount is valid until published on the matching event page.
06
After a matchResults and disputes
  • Report an issue in the existing WhatsApp chat within 15 minutes of the posted result.
  • Include the registration reference, match number, and clear screenshot or recording evidence.
  • The organizer reviews available evidence consistently and publishes corrections.
  • Abusive or repeated unsupported claims may be closed without further review.
07
Money and cancellationsPayments and rewards
A screenshot is not confirmation.

The organizer verifies incoming funds in the organizer-controlled account before assigning a number and verifies match results before paying rewards.

  • Pay only the entry fee displayed on the selected Registration open match.
  • A complete submission requires the correct transaction reference and a JPG, PNG, or WebP payment screenshot no larger than 2 MB.
  • The registration record is created only after all required fields pass validation and the private screenshot upload succeeds.
  • Do not pay while a match says Coming soon, Scheduled, Completed, or Registration closed.
  • Pay only by UPI to 9900344144@ybl, exactly as displayed on the registration page.
  • Verify the UPI ID and recipient before authorizing payment; never enter a UPI PIN on this website.
  • If QW cancels a match, verified entry payments follow the terms published for that match.
  • No-show, late arrival, rule removal, or incorrect submitted details are not normally refundable.
08
Player protectionPrivacy and account safety
  • After every required confirmation, the registration page uploads the complete lineup and payment proof to private Supabase storage and records the pending submission.
  • Only approved organizers can list private registrations or load payment screenshots.
  • After consent and organizer confirmation, the public roster may include only the registration reference, selected lobby, squad name, in-game names, Free Fire UIDs, assigned number, and status.
  • The match-group message contains only the reference, tournament, lobby, squad name when applicable, in-game names, and Free Fire UIDs.
  • Ages, email addresses, phone numbers, transaction references, payment proof, payment addresses, Supabase identifiers, room credentials, and organizer notes stay private.
  • Custom Room details are returned only when the entered private email matches a confirmed registration for that exact current match.
  • QW never asks for an OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, Free Fire password, account password, or identity document.
