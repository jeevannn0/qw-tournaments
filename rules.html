Baseline ruleset

Rules that protect the lobby

Event notices can add map, equipment, or round details. They cannot quietly change the published fee, prize, or refund terms.

01
Before registrationEligibility and lineup
  • Use the exact in-game name and Free Fire UID entered during registration.
  • Solo entries contain one player. Squad entries contain exactly four players and no substitute.
  • One player may appear in only one entry per tournament.
  • Players under 18 need parent or guardian approval for participation and any payment.
  • A generated reference is not a reservation. Only written organizer confirmation assigns a slot.
02
During competitionFair play and accounts
  • Hacks, scripts, modified clients, macros, teaming, account sharing, and deliberate bug exploitation are prohibited.
  • Use only the device and control methods allowed by the event notice. Emulators require explicit organizer approval.
  • Offensive names, harassment, threats, or abusive messages can result in removal.
  • The organizer may request match screenshots or screen recordings while reviewing credible evidence.
03
Before the lobbyCheck-in and room access
  • Check in through the existing organizer WhatsApp conversation 30 minutes before the scheduled start.
  • Room ID and password are sent privately to confirmed solo players or squad captains.
  • Do not share room credentials. Leaking credentials can cancel an entry without a refund.
  • Occupy the assigned slot and be ready before the announced lobby-close time.
  • Individual connection or device failure does not guarantee a restart.
04
Battle RoyalePlacement and elimination scoring

Unless an event page states otherwise, every elimination earns 1 point. Placement points follow this table:

Ties resolve by total wins, then eliminations, then placement points, then best final-match placement.

05
Clash Squad / TDMSeries format
  • Standard series are best of 3; finals may be best of 5 as displayed on the event page.
  • Map order, side selection, allowed equipment, and lobby settings are announced before check-in closes.
  • A team not ready by the lobby deadline may forfeit the map or series.
  • Only an organizer-confirmed lobby or server-wide failure can trigger a restart.
06
After a matchResults and disputes
  • Report an issue in the existing WhatsApp conversation within 15 minutes of the posted result.
  • Include the registration reference, match number, and clear screenshot or recording evidence.
  • The organizer reviews available evidence consistently and posts any correction publicly.
  • Abusive or repeated unsupported claims may be closed without further review.
07
Money and cancellationsPayments and refunds
A screenshot is not confirmation.

The organizer verifies incoming funds in the organizer-controlled account before assigning a slot.

  • Pay only using details sent from the WhatsApp number displayed on this website.
  • If QW cancels an event, verified entry payments follow the published return process.
  • No-show, late arrival, rule removal, or incorrect submitted details are not normally refundable.
  • If an event fills before payment verification, the organizer must offer a clearly agreed replacement or return the verified payment.
08
Player protectionPrivacy and account safety
  • The registration page prepares a WhatsApp message on your device; it does not upload your form.
  • Only approved in-game names, UIDs, team, slot, and status may appear publicly.
  • Ages, phone numbers, payment proof, payment addresses, and identity documents stay private.
  • QW never asks for an OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, Free Fire password, or account password.
