Fair play first

Tournament rules

These baseline rules cover solo, full-map squad, and TDM events. Event-specific WhatsApp notices may add map or round details but cannot quietly change fees or prizes.

I understand — register now →
01 Eligibility and registration
  • Players must use the exact in-game name and Free Fire UID submitted during registration.
  • Solo entries cover one player. Squad entries require exactly four players; no substitute is included.
  • One player may appear in only one entry per tournament.
  • Players under 18 need parent or guardian approval for participation and any payment.
  • A slot is confirmed only after organizer verification and a written WhatsApp reply.
02 Fair play and accounts
  • Hacks, scripts, modified clients, macros, teaming, account sharing, and exploiting known bugs are prohibited.
  • Use the device and control methods allowed in the event notice. Emulators are not allowed unless the event explicitly says otherwise.
  • Offensive names, harassment, threats, and abusive messages can lead to removal.
  • The organizer may request match screenshots or screen recordings when reviewing credible evidence.
03 Check-in and room access
  • Check in through the organizer's WhatsApp conversation 30 minutes before the scheduled start.
  • Room ID and password are sent privately to confirmed solo players or squad captains.
  • Do not share room credentials. Leaking them can cancel the entry without a refund.
  • Players must occupy their assigned slot and be ready before the announced lobby close time.
  • Late arrival or an individual connection/device issue does not guarantee a restart.
04 Battle Royale scoring

Unless the event notice states otherwise, each elimination earns 1 point. Placement points are:

Ties are resolved by total wins, then eliminations, then placement points, then the best final-match placement.

05 Clash Squad / TDM
  • Standard rounds are best of 3; the final may be best of 5 as shown on the event card.
  • Map order, side selection, allowed equipment, and lobby settings are announced before check-in closes.
  • A team that is not ready by the lobby deadline may forfeit the map or series.
  • Only an organizer-confirmed lobby or server-wide failure can trigger a restart.
06 Results and disputes
  • Report a result issue in the existing WhatsApp conversation within 15 minutes of the posted result.
  • Include the registration ID, match number, and clear screenshot or recording evidence.
  • The organizer reviews available evidence consistently and posts any correction publicly.
  • Abusive or repeated unsupported claims may be closed without further review.
07 Payments, cancellations, and refunds
  • Pay only using details sent from the WhatsApp number shown on this website.
  • Payment screenshots support a review but do not prove that funds reached the organizer.
  • If QW Tournaments cancels an event, verified entry payments are returned using the announced process.
  • No-show, late arrival, rule removal, or incorrect submitted details are not normally refundable.
  • If an event fills before payment is verified, the organizer must offer a place in a clearly agreed replacement event or return the verified payment.
08 Privacy and safety
  • The site does not submit your form to a server; it prepares a WhatsApp message on your device.
  • Only approved in-game names, UIDs, teams, slots, and match status may appear publicly.
  • Ages, phone numbers, payment proof, payment addresses, and identity documents must stay private.
  • QW Tournaments never asks for an OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, Free Fire password, or account password.

Before you join

Common questions

Still unsure? Message the organizer directly and mention the tournament name.

Ask on WhatsApp
Does submitting the form reserve my slot?

No. It prepares your registration message. Your slot is final only after payment is independently verified and the organizer replies with confirmation.

How do I send the payment screenshot?

After WhatsApp opens, send the prepared message first. Follow the organizer's verified payment instructions, then attach the screenshot directly in that chat.

Where will I receive the room ID and password?

Confirmed solo players or squad captains receive room credentials privately in WhatsApp before the match. Credentials are never published on this website.

Can I register fewer than four squad players?

No. Squad Battle Royale and squad TDM registration require exactly four player names, UIDs, and ages.

When does my name appear in the confirmed list?

The organizer publishes the final public roster about two hours before the match. Only approved public game details are shown.

Does the site save my form?

No. Personal registration fields are used only to prepare the WhatsApp message in your browser and are not stored by this website.
